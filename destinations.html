Explore Metro Manila

From historic landmarks to open green spaces and waterfront views, discover the places that make the capital unique.

Intramuros

Intramuros is a historic walled city in Manila. It is known for its old buildings, churches, and Spanish colonial history.

Location: Manila, Metro Manila

Rizal Park

Rizal Park is one of the famous parks in Manila. It is a historical place dedicated to Philippine national hero Jose Rizal.

Location: Manila, Metro Manila

Quiapo Church

Quiapo Church is known for the Black Nazarene, a famous statue of Jesus Christ. Visitors can pray, attend Mass, and learn about Filipino Catholic traditions. It is a popular destination because many devotees visit the church to pray, show their faith, and join the annual Feast of the Black Nazarene.

Location: Manila, Metro Manila
